# Data Verification Policy & Official Sources

ScholarPath enforces an anti-fabrication standard across every country profile, university profile, and scholarship entry.

## Verification Labels

- **`officially_verified`**: Directly checked against official government, regional DSU agency, or university admissions/financial aid portals. Every verified record stores `lastVerifiedAt` and direct `officialScholarshipUrl` / `officialApplicationUrl` links.
- **`needs_reverification`**: Flagged for periodic call renewal checks.
- **`unverified`**: Pending curator review (e.g., draft import).
- **`archived`**: Expired/discontinued programme hidden from default public discovery.
- **`synthetic_demo`**: Explicit demonstration/edge-case entries created for testing nationality disqualifiers or admin CSV imports. Clearly labeled `Synthetic Demo` in UI badges and excluded when *Official records only* is selected.

## Indicative Estimates Notice

Monthly living expenses shown in Country Guides (`livingCostMonthlyMin` – `livingCostMonthlyMax`) and Financial Calculator defaults combine immigration subsistence benchmarks (such as Germany's *Sperrkonto* guidance or Norway UDI baselines) with indicative student budget estimates. Always consult the official university and immigration portals before remitting funds or preparing visa financial proofs.
